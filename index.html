Behind the Booth

Ten Years of One Continuous Set

It started in church booths, learning to read a room that came to worship, not just to dance. That instinct — build the energy, never break the reverence — is still the whole method. Every tape starts on the same bedrock and lets the rest of the world in around it.

Based in the US now, HoWz Studios behind him, DJ Wealth still builds every set the same way: track by track, for the room that's actually in front of him, whether that's a sanctuary, a reception hall, or a festival stage.
