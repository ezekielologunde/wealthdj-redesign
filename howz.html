One Point of View, Every Medium

HoWz started with a DJ booth in a church in Ilorin, Nigeria — learning to read a room, build energy, and tell a story in real time. That instinct didn't stay in the booth. It became a mixtape catalog, then a camera, then a design practice, then a small studio building sites and systems.

Every division under HoWz — WealthDJ, Photography, Design, Branding, Cinema — is the same craft pointed at a different medium: pay attention to the room, then build something that belongs in it.
